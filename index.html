Our Mandate

Apex is built for long-term creation, ownership, development, and expansion rather than short-term production alone.

Entertainment

Apex is responsible for creating and developing entertainment across film, animation, music, games, publishing, interactive experiences, and future forms of entertainment.

Intellectual Property

Apex develops original characters, worlds, stories, brands, music, technologies, experiences, and other intellectual property with potential for continued use and expansion.

Creative Businesses

Apex builds specialized divisions and businesses capable of turning creative capabilities and intellectual property into sustainable long-term enterprises.

Talent & Capabilities

Apex develops the people, creative disciplines, technical capabilities, production systems, and leadership required to sustain its businesses.

Technology & Innovation

Apex develops and adopts technology where it strengthens creation, production, distribution, interaction, business operations, or future opportunities.

Expansion

Apex remains capable of entering new industries, mediums, markets, and business categories as opportunities and capabilities develop.

The mandate is not simply to produce. It is to build.
