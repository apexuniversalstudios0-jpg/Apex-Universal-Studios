Writing to us

To help the right person see your message, begin your subject line with the topic, such as one of the following:

  • General enquiry
  • Media and press
  • Partnership or collaboration
  • Business opportunity

Please include:

  • your name, and your organization if you represent one;
  • a clear statement of the purpose of your message;
  • any relevant links, in place of large attachments;
  • the best way to reply to you.

Verifying official communication

The email address and social accounts on this page are the official channels of Apex Universal Studios™. If you receive a message that claims to come from Apex through any other address or account, treat it with caution and confirm it here before you respond or share information.
