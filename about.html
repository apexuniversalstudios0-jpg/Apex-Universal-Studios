About Apex

Apex Universal Studios™ is the parent company built to create, own, develop, operate, and expand a portfolio of entertainment, media, technology, intellectual property, and creative businesses across multiple industries and future mediums.

Who we areWhat we doWhere we're headedHow we thinkDivisionsWhat we stand for

Who we are

Apex is the larger company and ecosystem. Its divisions are specialized expressions of it, and none of them defines Apex. Animation is not Apex, and neither is music. Each division can keep its own identity, expertise, and creative practices while remaining part of one company.

Apex is built on seven principles:

  • Parent Company
  • Creative Enterprise
  • Long-Term Ownership
  • Multi-Industry
  • Ecosystem Thinking
  • Distinctive Divisions
  • Continuous Expansion

What we do

Apex exists to create, develop, and operate exceptional entertainment, creative businesses, intellectual property, and technology that connect with audiences and create lasting value. We do that through six connected activities.

CreateDevelop entertainment, stories, music, games, software, experiences, and other creative work.
DevelopTurn ideas, talent, capabilities, and intellectual property into stronger and more complete creative and commercial assets.
OperateBuild and maintain businesses capable of producing, managing, distributing, and expanding Apex's work.
ConnectCreate work and experiences that reach and engage audiences across relevant mediums and platforms.
InnovateUse technology, new methods, and emerging opportunities to strengthen creation and expand what Apex can build.
EndureBuild work, businesses, and IP with the potential to remain valuable and relevant over the long term.

The mission: create what matters, build what lasts, and continuously expand what Apex is capable of creating.

Where we're headed

Apex aims to become a globally recognized, enduring creative enterprise built around exceptional entertainment, powerful intellectual property, innovative technology, and a diverse ecosystem of businesses.

The vision is directional, not a forecast. It doesn't promise a set number of divisions, and it doesn't require every idea to become a franchise. The shape of future expansion is intentionally open.

The vision is not to become one kind of company. It is to build a company capable of creating what comes next.

How we think

Apex believes exceptional creative work and enduring businesses are built through intentional creation, strong ownership, disciplined execution, continuous development, and long-term thinking. Ten principles guide it:

  • Create With Purpose
  • Build, Don't Just Produce
  • Creativity & Business Work Together
  • Think Long Term
  • Own What We Build
  • Develop Continuously
  • Specialization Strengthens the Ecosystem
  • Technology Serves Creation
  • Quality Requires Discipline
  • Adapt Without Losing the Core

Apex treats creation as a process of development, from discovery and concept through production and release to continued development. It treats intellectual property as both creative expression and long-term value, and it doesn't assume every property should become a large franchise.

Our divisions

The current Apex ecosystem includes:

Apex Animation Studio™Animated films, series, and shorts. Apex Pictures™Comics, graphic novels, and publishing. Apex Music™Music creation, soundtracks, and albums. Apex Labels™Artist development, releases, and catalog management. Apex Games™Games and interactive entertainment. Apex Software™Software, digital products, and creative technology.

Additional divisions may be established as Apex grows.

What we stand for

  • Originality
  • Creative Excellence
  • Ambition
  • Meaningful Entertainment
  • Long-Term Thinking
  • Ownership
  • Innovation
  • Adaptability
  • Collaboration
  • Respect for the Work

Apex does not simply seek to make more. It seeks to build better, build deeper, and build what can last.
