Our Mandate

Apex Universal Studios™ exists to build enduring entertainment businesses, original worlds, memorable characters, valuable intellectual property, and creative products that can grow across generations and mediums.

The mandatePrinciplesSix areasResponsibilitiesDivisionsOwnershipBoundaries

The mandate

Apex is built for long-term creation, ownership, development, and expansion rather than short-term production alone.

The mandate goes beyond individual projects. Apex is responsible for building a durable creative and commercial ecosystem that can produce value across multiple businesses, mediums, and generations.

Seven principles

Build for PermanenceCreate businesses, intellectual property, and creative assets with long-term potential.
Create OriginalityDevelop original worlds, characters, stories, experiences, technologies, and other intellectual property.
Develop ContinuouslyTreat ideas and intellectual property as assets capable of continued development rather than one-time outputs.
Own What Is BuiltPursue meaningful ownership and control of the company's original creative assets and businesses.
Expand Across MediumsAllow successful ideas and capabilities to grow across appropriate entertainment, media, technology, and future mediums.
Build BusinessesDevelop sustainable companies and business capabilities around creative work.
Protect Long-Term ValueMake decisions that preserve the identity, quality, usefulness, and future potential of Apex's assets.

Six areas of the mandate

The mandate operates across six interconnected areas.

Entertainment

Apex is responsible for creating and developing entertainment across film, animation, music, games, publishing, interactive experiences, and future forms of entertainment.

Intellectual Property

Apex develops original characters, worlds, stories, brands, music, technologies, experiences, and other intellectual property with potential for continued use and expansion.

Creative Businesses

Apex builds specialized divisions and businesses capable of turning creative capabilities and intellectual property into sustainable long-term enterprises.

Talent & Capabilities

Apex develops the people, creative disciplines, technical capabilities, production systems, and leadership required to sustain its businesses.

Technology & Innovation

Apex develops and adopts technology where it strengthens creation, production, distribution, interaction, business operations, or future opportunities.

Expansion

Apex remains capable of entering new industries, mediums, markets, and business categories as opportunities and capabilities develop.

What the mandate covers

The mandate applies across the entire Apex ecosystem. Apex is responsible for:

  • creating and developing original work;
  • building enduring intellectual property;
  • establishing and operating specialized businesses;
  • developing long-term creative and commercial capabilities;
  • protecting and increasing the value of company-owned assets;
  • creating opportunities for intellectual property and businesses to expand;
  • establishing new divisions and ventures when appropriate;
  • maintaining the long-term direction of the parent company.

How each division contributes

  • Apex Animation Studio™ contributes through animated entertainment and related creative capabilities.
  • Apex Pictures™ contributes through comics, graphic novels, publishing, illustrated storytelling, and visual intellectual property.
  • Apex Music™ contributes through music, soundtracks, albums, releases, and musical projects.
  • Apex Labels™ contributes through artist development, label operations, catalog development, and the broader music business.
  • Apex Games™ contributes through games, interactive entertainment, and related experiences.
  • Apex Software™ contributes through software, digital products, creative technology, and technology-based businesses.

These divisions do not have to perform the same work. Their responsibility is to contribute their specialized capabilities to the larger Apex mandate.

Long-term creation and ownership

A central part of the mandate is building assets that can outlive individual projects. A project may succeed as a single film, series, album, game, comic, software product, or other release. The broader question is whether the underlying ideas, intellectual property, capabilities, talent, and business created around that work can support continued development.

Apex therefore approaches creation with the possibility of long-term ownership, development, adaptation, expansion, and reuse where appropriate. Intellectual property may take many forms:

  • Characters
  • Worlds
  • Stories
  • Franchises
  • Brands
  • Music
  • Technologies
  • Software
  • Games
  • Publishing
  • Experiences

The company may expand intellectual property across divisions and mediums when doing so strengthens the work and preserves its long-term value.

What the mandate is not

The mandate does not require Apex to pursue every available medium, industry, technology, or commercial opportunity. Expansion must stay connected to the company's larger purpose and capabilities.

It does not require every project to become a franchise, every division to expand indefinitely, or every creative work to serve multiple mediums. It establishes a long-term direction, not a requirement for artificial expansion.

Apex Universal Studios™ builds for the long term. The mandate is not simply to produce. It is to build.
